# Metric Definitions and Analytical Scope

The analysis uses `project.games_paid_users` and `project.games_payments`. The Tableau extract is produced at **user × calendar month** granularity for March–December 2022. Raw user identifiers are not published.

| Metric | Calculation / Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Revenue (MRR proxy) | Sum of recorded payments during a calendar month |
| Paid Users | Distinct users who made a payment in the month |
| ARPPU | Monthly payment revenue divided by paid users |
| New Paid Users / New MRR | First observed paying month and revenue generated by these users |
| Reactivated Users / Reactivation MRR | Prior payers with no payment in the immediately preceding month, who paid again |
| Churned Users / Churned Revenue | Previous-month payers with no payment in the current month; lost revenue uses their previous-month payment total |
| Churn Rate | Churned users / previous-month paid users |
| Revenue Churn Rate | Churned revenue / previous-month revenue |
| Expansion MRR | Positive revenue change among consecutively paying users |
| Contraction MRR | Negative revenue change among consecutively paying users |
| Observed Lifetime (LT) | Inclusive number of months between first and last observed payment within the sample |
| Observed LTV | Total observed revenue per user, averaged across all observed paying users |

## Monthly Reconciliation

**Paid users:** Previous paid users + new users + reactivated users − churned users = current paid users.

**Revenue:** Previous revenue + new MRR + reactivation MRR + expansion MRR − contraction MRR − churned revenue = current revenue.

**March 2022:** The data has no preceding month, so churn rates are not defined.

## Methodological Limitations

- The course uses the term **MRR**, but the underlying data contains payment transactions rather than verified subscription contracts. Values should therefore be interpreted as **monthly payment revenue**, not independently validated subscription MRR.
- Churn indicates absence of a payment in a particular month and can be followed by reactivation; it is not necessarily permanent account cancellation.
- The first and last observed months limit lifetime estimates. Observed LT/LTV are descriptive, not predictive or complete lifetime estimates.
- SQL is written for the authorized GoIT training database. It cannot be executed without access to the two source tables.
- For public sharing, only monthly aggregated values, SQL logic, and derived visualizations are included; transaction-level user records are not published.
